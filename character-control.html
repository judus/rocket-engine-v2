Walk with WASD. Hold Shift to run.

Click either canvas to focus it, then use W/A/S/D. Diagonal movement is normalized. The brown wall and purple unknown terrain block swept movement; go around the wall or mark the purple patch known. The cyan circle marks the character's ground clearance.

Running moves 1.7× faster and plays the original walk clip at 1.5× speed. There is no separate run animation. Stopping or pushing into a blocked area returns to directional idle. Both views share one character and one fixed-step update owner. This demo has no autonomous work or CFW dependency.
