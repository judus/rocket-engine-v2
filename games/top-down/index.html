Salvage courtyard

Move with WASD or arrows. Shift runs. F collects nearby supplies. Click the viewport to activate controls.

You and the scout share movement, body, inventory and interaction capabilities. The scout supplies its own intent. Collect pale green supply cells inside your dashed interaction ring. Walls block movement and collection. Full bags leave supplies in place. Removing the scout retires its bag; a new scout starts empty.
