FARWAKE / SETTINGS

Settings

Flight is paused. Changes take effect when you apply them.

Bindings

Select a binding to replace it, or + to add an alternative. Escape cancels a waiting binding; otherwise it returns to the menu. C or right mouse toggles mouse steering; both are reserved.

Rendering resolution

Lower resolution reduces GPU work. Menus stay sharp at every setting.

Render scale applies after the density limit. The limit prevents high-density screens from rendering unnecessary pixels.

Surface detail

Full preserves all surface detail. No normal maps removes fine surface relief. Scalar PBR also replaces material maps with representative values to reduce GPU memory use.

Applies when you resume flight. Switching may cause a brief pause while graphics resources rebuild.

Flight behaviour, field of view and controls stay the same. Hardware limits may further reduce the requested resolution.
