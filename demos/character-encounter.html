Restore the watchtower route.

Focus the canvas. WASD moves, Shift runs. E or a nearby click defeats a sentry, talks to the watchkeeper, collects a seal or delivers it to a beacon. Clear the three courtyard sentries, approach the watchkeeper and choose blue or red. Blue goes to Beacon Ridge A (north); red goes to Beacon Ridge B (south).

The quest starts automatically from the session JSON. Every stage, route and consequence below is executed by CFW without AI. Your dialogue choice selects the story branch. Only a matching seal delivery completes it. A matching delivery made before your choice also counts.
