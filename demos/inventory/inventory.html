ONE INVENTORY AUTHORITY

Objects hold inventories.

A player bag uses slots. A house shares a quantity limit. A lake holds a finite supply of water.

Take water from the lake, then store it in the house. Full transfers leave both inventories unchanged.

The lake does not refill automatically. Each bulk stack holds up to 5 units in the bag; individual tools use one slot each. The house also has a separate tool cabinet.
