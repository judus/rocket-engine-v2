The pool holds at most 4,096 particles. Overload drops emission attempts with bounded work; it never creates 100,000 nodes or physics bodies. Constant acceleration, lifetime fade and linear size interpolation run on Rocket’s existing fixed clock. Pausing freezes simulation; disposal releases both views, input, emitters and storage.
