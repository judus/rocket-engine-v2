ENGINE EFFECTS · 02 / PROJECTILES

Fast shots. No skipped targets.

Point projectiles sweep their complete fixed-step path against eligible static targets. Trails and impact sparks use the separate particle pool. Aim with the pointer; click or hold Space to fire.

Green and blue targets are only 4 pixels thick. At 20,000 px/s a shot travels 333 pixels per 60 Hz step; endpoint-only overlap would miss them. Sweeping selects the earliest hit, retires the shot and returns a record. The demo handles that record by emitting sparks; there is no damage system.

First 2D milestone: point shots against static axis-aligned rectangles; no moving-target relative sweep, radius, ricochet, piercing, networking or damage rules. Rendering is separate from simulation. API and ownership · Particle emitter
