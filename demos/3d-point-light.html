Reading the metrics

FPS, frame intervals and CPU times come from Rocket’s rolling half-second monitor. Peak is the longest frame interval in that window. Frame jitter is the standard deviation of those intervals; lower means more even pacing. A very short minimum alongside a much longer peak indicates callbacks arriving in bursts. CPU timings measure JavaScript work; they do not measure GPU execution or browser compositing. Lines count the current frame after / before clipping.

For comparisons, keep the same canvas size, display scale, camera pose and field of view, and keep this tab in the foreground. FPS is capped by the browser/display refresh rate; CPU times can increase before FPS drops. Recreate demo starts a fresh measurement window.

Twice the distance gives one quarter of the direct illumination when the surface angle is unchanged. Close distances are clamped at 0.1 units to avoid an infinite hotspot. Strength is a preview multiplier, not a calibrated physical unit.

Flat shading: one sample at the average vertex position of each face. The tiled floor reveals the falloff; choose one large face to see why coarse geometry gives coarse lighting. No cast shadows: walls and floors do not block light. Very strong values clip toward white.

Starting…

Click or Tab into the canvas. WASD moves across the ground plane; Q / E lowers / raises the camera. Drag or arrow keys to look, Shift moves faster, R resets. Moving focus out of the canvas releases keyboard control.

Flat Lambert-style diffuse only: no cast shadows, specular highlights, metallic/roughness response, smooth shading, textures or WebGL. Milestones and boundaries
