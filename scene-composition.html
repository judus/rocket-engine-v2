The bridge keeps its position and camera. Every hangar visit starts a new instance. During loading the old scene stays interactive. Switch again to cancel a slow load. After a failed load, click Hangar to retry. Keys held across a switch must be released and pressed again.

Both scenes have their own camera, world layers and screen HUD. One canvas, one Rocket loop. Dispose releases every scene and input binding; the final created/disposed counts must match.
