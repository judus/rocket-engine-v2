ROCKET / CHARACTER FRAMEWORK

Mara and the Old Grove

Scripted dialogue, real CFW quests, Rocket movement. No live AI calls.

  1. Player visit: click Walk to Old Grove or click inside the green grove area, and wait for arrival there. Click Return to Mara, open her chat and send “hello”. Mara acknowledges the recorded visit. Walking there freely does not create or complete a quest.
  2. Player quest: send “work”, then choose the CFW offer's accept option. Use Walk to Old Grove or click inside the green grove and wait for arrival; the active quest completes only when the selected scenario's game-owned visit condition is satisfied. Send “hello” for the completion reply. In the default scenario, earlier visits do not count: leave the grove and arrive again after accepting. In the alternate scenario, an earlier visit prevents the offer.
  3. Mara's journey: send exactly “go to grove”. Mara walks around the wall herself and replies after arrival, cancellation or an unreachable outcome. Send “hello” after she arrives; she acknowledges her own arrival. Her journey does not complete the player's quest.

Both chats share the same host state. This is a scripted local proof, not a live AI conversation. Reload starts a fresh session and clears visits, journeys and quests.

To test recovery, click Lose next departure reply before sending “go to grove”. Wait for the injected error after the journey settles, then click Retry reply in chat. The original outcome is replayed without another journey. Fail next scripted reply fails before an action starts.

Stop waiting, closing a chat and closing the diagnostic view preserve movement. Cancel Mara journey cancels game movement; Retry Mara route retries an existing blocked route. Remove Mara retires the actor and prevents new journeys. Dispose proof stops and disposes the whole demo. Reload to start again.
