Ship commands

Dampers off: commands request thrust and neutral coasts. Dampers on: commands request assisted speed and angular rates; neutral brakes drift.

Dampers off: rotation retains angular momentum. Pitch up and bank right are positive. Attitude angles wrap; quaternion orientation permits full loops. Try a starting speed of 12,300 m/s, Reset, then enable dampers to inspect capture and its power limits. Capture first reduces speed to the assisted limit along the entry trajectory; afterward current commands take over. Reset creates a new ship; ordinary control and module switches preserve the current ship and reserve.

Ship navigation

        

          Known lab beacons in a fixed world frame, measured in metres. Select a
          destination, then Run or Single step. The ship-forward scope covers 10
          km; distant destinations remain selectable. Guidance only: sensor
          detection, navigation power/health and autopilot are not modeled yet.
        

        

        
Ship computer

        

          One committed module overview and command router. Choose a module and
          switch it on/off, then Run or Single step. Warnings show current
          faults, reserve at or below 10%, power shortages and requested
          factories without operators. Production state comes directly from
          Workshop. The computer itself has no power or health simulation in
          this first slice.

Ship internals · production & crew facilities

        

          Refinery: 2 ore → 1 alloy in 4 powered seconds. Fabricator: 2 alloy →
          1 machine part in 3 seconds. Galley: 2 ingredients → 1 meal in 3
          seconds. Science lab: 1 machine part → 1 science point in 6 powered
          seconds. Infirmary: 2 medical supplies → 1 health pack in 4 seconds.
          Quarters: powered accommodation; crew fatigue recovery comes later.
          Fridge: 60 units of food storage; no spoilage model yet. Every factory
          needs one operator. Any crew member works; matching specialists give
          +25% productivity: engineer for refinery/fabricator, scientist for the
          science lab, physician for infirmary, navigator for galley. Select a
          slot, assign a worker, supply lab stock and enable it, then Run.
          Transfer goods between buffers using the resource, quantity and
          destination controls. Outages preserve active work. Drain to finish
          without starting another batch; collect all stock, disable and cool
          before removal. Lab installation and supply are free. Stock is in a
          local buffer, independent of the interdimensional cargo link.
          Occupancy and save/restore come later.
        

        

        
Engine condition · lab controls

        

          
        

        

          Applies on the next simulation step. These lab commands bypass shields
          and hull; repair costs no resources. Damage reduces thrust and
          rotational capacity. Zero health disables the engine; it still
          remembers its requested switch. Reactor health is modeled below; other
          module health comes later.
        

        
Reactor condition · lab controls

        

          
        

        

          Reactor damage reduces generation. Failure stops generation and
          starter draw, but preserves stored reserve energy. Repair needs a
          restart and cannot bypass an existing thermal or emergency reboot.
          These lab commands apply on the next step and cost no resources.
        

        
Cooling condition · lab controls

        

          
        

        

          Damage reduces the shared circuit's removal capacity. Failure stops
          pump demand and active cooling; reactor passive cooling continues.
          Repair restores capacity on the next step when the pump is requested
          on and funded. These lab repairs cost no resources.
        

        
Damper condition · lab controls

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

          Damage weakens assistance and crew protection. Failure stops damper
          power demand and leaves momentum intact. Repair respects startup and
          thermal reboot; capture resumes from actual velocity. Lab repairs
          apply on the next step and cost no resources.
        

        
Shield condition · lab controls

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

          Damage reduces recharge efficiency and the fraction of each hit
          absorbed; the remainder reaches the hull. Failure stops the field and
          recharge, preserving stored energy for emergency diversion. Repair
          respects startup and thermal reboot. These lab commands apply next
          step and cost no resources.
        

        
Cargo link · lab controls

        

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

          Link failure or manual shutdown starts one recovery window. Restore
          funded, cool operation and complete stable reconnection before expiry.
          Brief power or switch toggles cannot reset the countdown. This lab
          tracks a pocket reference, not an inventory or cargo mass.
        

        

          
        

        
        
Shields & hull

        
        
        

        

        

          
        

        

        

          Diversion switches shields off and needs no reactor power. Run or
          Single step to transfer stored energy. It stops when shields empty or
          the reserve fills. Re-enable shields manually to restore protection;
          enabling them also cancels diversion.
        

        

          
        

        

          Hits apply on the next simulation step. Enable shields and Run to
          charge them. Switching off preserves energy but removes protection.
          Hull breach is reported; destruction rules come later.

Assembly

        
ship
├─ body       owns position, velocity and quaternion orientation
├─ control    accepts intent from any controller
├─ navigation post-motion position fix and destination guidance
├─ power      owns reserve and allocation
├─ engine     requests power, delivers thrust/torque
├─ dampers    powered assistance, capture and crew protection
├─ cargo      powered pocket access with a fixed recovery deadline
├─ mounts     external installation slots and fire-group matrix
├─ internals  replaceable installations bound to Workshop facilities/inventories
├─ cooling    one cooling budget across all thermal modules
├─ shields    stored field energy and powered protection
├─ hull       shield overflow becomes structural damage
├─ movement   orders one fixed simulation step
└─ computer   reads the committed frame and routes module commands
