Ship commands

Dampers off: commands request thrust and neutral coasts. Dampers on: commands request assisted speed and yaw rate; neutral brakes drift.

Dampers off: yaw retains inertia. Try a starting speed of 12,300 m/s, Reset, then enable dampers to inspect capture and its power limits. Capture first reduces speed to the assisted limit along the entry trajectory; afterward current commands take over. Reset creates a new ship; ordinary control and module switches preserve the current ship and reserve.
