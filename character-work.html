Work → takeover → resume

Two operators walk to their workplaces. Select a sprite or choose a worker, then take control. Click the canvas and use WASD; hold Shift to run. Selection alone never interrupts work. Release control to resume the saved assignment.

Taking control immediately clears attendance and releases the slot. Workshop owns production and timed deliveries; these characters are operators, not physical cargo carriers. The press continues while it has alloy available.
